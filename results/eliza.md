# Eliza product requirements: results

The Eliza product map (`products/eliza.yaml`, 203 requirements) run by
`tools/run.py` at commit 56e3944 on two Eliza CQS EVKs and, with only
its board profile, on a Qualcomm RB3 Gen 2, and at 9bc9cee (5de9068 on
this branch, same tree) on a third Eliza CQS EVK. Every status links to the
log line that reports it. pass, partial, fail and skip are defined in
the top-level README; partial is reported to LAVA as "unknown".

Overlay: runner, tests, profiles and map at 56e3944 (EVK 02: 9bc9cee) plus the public
conformance vectors from `tools/fetch-vectors.sh`.

What the boards lack, and so where requirements skip: no monitor or
EDID dummy plug on HDMI and no DP sink on any of them, no DSI panel,
backlight or touch controller, no camera module on the Eliza EVKs used,
no calibrated audio analyzer. On EVK 04 both WSA8845 speaker amplifiers
were UNATTACHED on SoundWire during the run (soundwire case), so its
acoustic audio results fail for that board reason; EVK 03 attached one
amplifier. EVK 02 had both amplifiers UNATTACHED too; its four audio
skips beyond EVK 04 (AUD-008, AUD-023, AUD-044, AUD-045) are the
interfaces `boards/eliza-evk.yaml` now lists absent. The RB3 Gen 2
profile characterises no audio routes, so its acoustic and shell audio
cases skip with that reason.

Notes on single results:

- AUD-013 (compress offload) runs last on the Eliza EVK (after it,
  MultiMedia2 playback stays silent until the next boot). Its playback
  sub-cases failed on EVK 03, after the DSP restart test, and on EVK 04,
  which had no speaker amplifier attached; in an earlier development
  run on EVK 03 with the same image (job 594711, uncommitted tree) it
  passed 6 of 6 sub-cases. Not investigated yet.
- AUD-062 (soft stepping) failed on EVK 03 by the test's minimum: the
  volume step lowered the tone by 19.5 dB and the test analyses the ramp
  only for a drop of 20 dB or more. The threshold is to be revisited
  before reading this as a feature result.
- AUD-053 on the Eliza EVKs: no qcom_stats directory on this kernel; on
  RB3 Gen 2 the island counters did not advance in 10 s of idle.
